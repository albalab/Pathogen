How to use

This standalone page replicates the Pathogen genetic algorithm visualization without external dependencies.

  • Drag inside the maze to draw walls. Hold Shift while dragging to erase.
  • Drag the green (start) or red (goal) nodes to reposition them.
  • Use the Controls button to open sliders for maze size, population, and mutation rate.
  • Use the toolbar buttons to pause, resume, or fast-forward the simulation.
  • The lightning button fast-forwards until a solution is found or the step cap is reached.
